About DirectFarm LK

DirectFarm LK is a revolutionary platform connecting Sri Lankan farmers directly with consumers, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair prices for everyone.

Our mission is to empower local farmers, provide fresh produce to consumers, and support sustainable agriculture practices across Sri Lanka.


For Farmers

Sell directly to consumers and get fair prices for your hard work.

For Consumers

Access fresh, locally-sourced produce at competitive prices.
