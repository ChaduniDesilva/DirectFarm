Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about DirectFarm LK

How does DirectFarm LK work?
DirectFarm LK connects local farmers directly with consumers, ensuring fresher produce and better prices for everyone involved.
Is there a delivery fee?
Delivery fees vary based on your location and the specific farmer's settings. You can see the fee at checkout.
How do I know the produce is fresh?
We prioritize same-day harvesting and direct delivery from farm to table.
Can I return or exchange products?
Yes, we have a 24-hour return policy for quality-related issues.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and cash on delivery.
How do I become a seller/farmer on DirectFarm LK?
Click on the 'Register' button and select 'Farmer' to start your onboarding process.
What areas do you deliver to?
Currently, we cover the Western and Central provinces, with plans to expand nationwide.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our customer support team is here to help.
