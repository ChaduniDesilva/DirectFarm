# 🌿 DirectFarm LK

**Connecting Farmers and Consumers Across Sri Lanka**

DirectFarm LK is a modern web platform designed to bridge the gap between local farmers and consumers. By eliminating traditional middlemen, the platform empowers Sri Lankan farmers to earn more while providing consumers with fresh, organic, and affordable produce.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Dashboard
A comprehensive management suite for farmers to digitize their operations.
* **Analytics Hub**: Track total revenue (e.g., Rs 328,000), total orders, and active customer growth with interactive sales graphs.
* **Inventory Management**: Manage product listings like Fresh Tomatoes and Organic Cabbage with real-time stock and price tracking.
* **Communications**: A built-in messaging system to communicate directly with customers.
* **Settings & Profile**: Manage farm descriptions, locations (e.g., Anuradhapura), and account security.

### 🛒 Marketplace & Cart
A customer-facing portal for seamless agricultural shopping.
* **Smart Filtering**: Browse crops by category (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Spices) and District (Nuwara Eliya, Matale, etc.).
* **Product Search**: Real-time search functionality to find specific crops instantly.
* **Shopping Cart**: Intuitive cart experience showing order summaries, delivery fees, and "free delivery" progress bars.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+).
* **Icons**: [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/) for UI elements.
* **Design Strategy**: Modern, clean UI/UX designed with a focus on usability for agricultural stakeholders.
